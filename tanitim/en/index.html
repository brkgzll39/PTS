Why OSTIVA

Ordinary plate recognition software reads plates. OSTIVA runs the gate.

The most common problem on site is the missing record nobody notices: an unread plate, a server that was off, a camera that stayed broken for weeks. OSTIVA catches all three on its own.

No missed passages

OSTIVA's own AI recognition and the camera's built-in plate recognition work together. Every night the records are compared with the camera's own log, and any missed passage is added with its real time.

camera 620 · OSTIVA 610 → 10 passages added

Nothing lost when the server is down

During a power cut or shutdown the camera keeps recording. When the system comes back it pulls the passages it missed from the camera, and raises an alarm if a blacklisted vehicle went through.

02:10–03:12 offline · 37 passages recovered

Faults found before you notice

Alerts for a camera that stopped reading plates, a filling disk, a failed backup or undelivered notifications. Our support team monitors every installation centrally; no plate or personal data is sent to us.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work with our existing cameras?

Yes. We read IP camera video with our own AI, and on Dahua ANPR cameras we also use the camera's built-in plate recognition. The site survey tells you which cameras are suitable.

Does our data leave the site?

No. OSTIVA runs on your own server and can be installed without internet access. If maintenance monitoring is enabled, only health data (are the cameras working, disk, backups) is sent; never plates, people or photos.

Which licence plates does it read?

It is in production with Turkish plates today. For other countries' plate formats we adapt and test the recognition during the pilot.

Do we need barriers and LED signs?

No. If you have them we connect your existing barrier and LED sign; if not, our partners can supply them turnkey.

How many gates and cameras does it support?

From a single gate to multi-gate campuses. Licences are per camera, with per-gate reports and permissions.
